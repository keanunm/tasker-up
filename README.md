## 🌟 Why Tasker Up?

Most to-do apps make you click through five different dropdown menus just to write down a single thought. **Tasker Up!** gets out of your way:

- **Type Like You Talk**: Just write a quick message or note, hit `Enter`, and it's on your list.
- **Smart Natural Shortcuts**: Want to file it right away? Just type `#Design`, `@Website`, or `!urgent` in your message. Tasker Up organizes it automatically.
- **Dedicated Project Folders**: Keep work projects, client tasks, and personal errands neatly separated.
- **Custom Color Categories**: Create your own vibrant categories with custom icons and accent colors.
- **Instant & Private**: Works directly in your browser. No account required, zero tracking, and your data stays safely stored on your device.

## ⚡ How to Use: Fast Message Entry

The quickest way to add any task is the **Fast Message Add** bar at the top of your screen:

```
Review landing page wireframes with Sarah #Design @"Website Redesign" !urgent ^tomorrow
```

### 🏷️ Magic Shortcuts Cheatsheet

You can use any of these shortcuts right in your message, or pick them with the clickable chips below the text box:

| Shortcut | What It Does | Example | Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`#category`** | Sets or creates a Category | `#Work`, `#Design`, `#Finance` | Color-coded category badge added |
| **`@folder`** | Files into a Project Folder | `@Marketing`, `@"App Launch"` | Filed directly into that project |
| **`!priority`** | Sets urgency level | `!urgent`, `!high`, `!med`, `!low` | Highlighted priority status dot |
| **`^date`** | Sets due date | `^today`, `^tomorrow`, `^2026-11-01` | Schedules calendar due date |

> **💡 Pro Tip:** If you type a `#category` or `@folder` that doesn't exist yet, Tasker Up **automatically creates it for you**!

## 📁 Organizing with Project Folders

Keep your workspace clean and organized by project:

1. Click **+ New** next to **Projects / Folders** in the left sidebar.
2. Choose a project name (e.g., *Client Alpha*, *Mobile Redesign*, *Personal Errands*).
3. Pick your favorite icon and color theme.
4. Click on any folder to instantly view only tasks belonging to that project!

---

## 🎨 Creating Custom Categories

Categories let you group related types of work across all your folders:

1. Click **+ New** next to **Categories** in the sidebar (or click the **+ New Category** chip in the entry bar).
2. Type your category name (e.g. *Security*, *Social Media*, *Fitness*).
3. Select an accent color from the glowing palette and pick a fun icon.
4. Watch the **Live Badge Preview** update in real-time, then click **Save Category**!

## 💎 Extra Features You'll Love

- **☁️ Cloud Login & Multi-Device Sync**: Sign in with Google or Email/Password. Your tasks, project folders, and categories automatically sync in real-time across your phone, tablet, and computer with offline support.
- **🎙️ Voice Dictation**: Click the microphone icon to speak your tasks aloud (Speech-to-Text).
- **🔊 Satisfying Audio Feedback**: Enjoy rewarding sound chimes when you check off items or create tasks (includes a quick mute toggle in the sidebar).
- **⭐ Star & Priority Pinning**: Star your most important items to pin them to the top of your workspace.
- **🔍 Instant Search & Multi-Sort**: Search across task names, notes, projects, and categories instantly. Sort by Newest, Oldest, Priority, Due Date, or Alphabetical.
- **💾 Backup & Portability**: Export your entire workspace to a JSON file anytime, or restore previous backups with one click.

- **Styling**: Vanilla CSS3 (Custom properties, glassmorphism, responsive grid & flexbox)

### Running Locally
To test or edit locally on your machine:
```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/keanunm/tasker-up.git

# Navigate into the folder
cd tasker-up

# Start any simple HTTP server (e.g., Python)
python -m http.server 5173
```
Then open `http://localhost:5173` in your browser.
